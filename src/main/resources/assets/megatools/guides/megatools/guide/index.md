# Mega Tools

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="megatools:stone_paxel" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="megatools:iron_paxel" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="megatools:diamond_paxel" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="megatools:mega_tool" scale="2" />
</Row>

* **Paxels**: pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and sword in one item.
* **Mega Tool**: breaks any block instantly and never wears out.
* Every tool has a menu with ability toggles, a torch slot, two Enchantment Plates and, from iron up,
  storage for your other tools.

Hold a tool and press <KeyBind id="key.megatools.open_gui" /> to open its menu.
