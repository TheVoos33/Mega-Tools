# Mega Tools

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="megatools:stone_paxel" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="megatools:iron_paxel" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="megatools:diamond_paxel" scale="2" />
  <ItemImage id="megatools:mega_tool" scale="2" />
</Row>

**Paxels** are a pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and sword in one item. The **Mega Tool** breaks any block
instantly and never wears out. Every tool has its own menu with ability toggles, a torch slot that
lights your way, swappable [Enchantment Plates](enchantment_plates.md) and, from iron upwards, storage
for your other tools.

Press <KeyBind id="key.megatools.open_gui" /> while holding a tool to open its menu, and click the **?**
button in its title bar to open this guide on the menu's page. Hover any Mega Tools item and hold
<KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" /> to jump to its page.
